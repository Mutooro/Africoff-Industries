Answers on Sourcing, Traceability and Direct Partnership

What coffee varieties and grades does AFRICOFF export? expand_more

We supply green specialty and commercial Arabica including Mount Elgon Bugisu (Grades AA, AB, PB, E), Rwenzori Natural Drugar, and Natural Uganda Robusta (NUR) in Screen 12, Screen 15, and Screen 18. All lots meet strict physical defect tolerances and moisture caps (10.5% - 11.5%).

How does AFRICOFF comply with the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR)? expand_more

All out-grower farms within our cooperative network are digitally surveyed with GPS polygon coordinates. We verify zero deforestation against Dec 31, 2020 baseline satellite data, providing full due diligence statements and downloadable GeoJSON dossiers for every container.

What are your minimum order quantities (MOQ) and freight terms? expand_more

Our standard export volume is 1 FCL (Full Container Load = approx. 320 x 60kg bags sealed in GrainPro hermetic liners). For specialty micro-lots (86+ SCA), we accommodate consolidated pallet air or ocean shipments under FOB Mombasa, CIF destination, or FOT (Free On Truck) Kampala terms.

How do smallholder farmers enroll in the Out-Growers Scheme? expand_more

Farmers with existing coffee plots in Mount Elgon, Rwenzori, or central Robusta regions can register with our field extension officers. Membership includes free agronomy training, soil testing, subsidized shade seedlings, and guaranteed off-take contracts at premium floor prices.
