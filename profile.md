## About Us

### Introduction

AFRICOFF INDUSTRIES (U) LIMITED is a registered agribusiness limited company in Uganda, specialising in the production, processing, value addition, and export of high-quality coffee and other agricultural products.

### Our Mission

To become a leading player in the Ugandan agricultural sector, while promoting gender balance, empowering women in agriculture, and improving the livelihoods of smallholder farmers through our out-growers scheme.

### Company Overview

Africoff Industries (U) Limited was founded in 2021 and was incorporated in 2025 under the Companies Act of Uganda.

Our headquarters are located in Kampala, Uganda. Our mission is to become a leading player in the Ugandan agricultural sector, while promoting sustainable agricultural practices, producing high-quality coffee, processing and marketing high-quality coffee products, and improving the livelihoods of our partner coffee growers/smallholder farmers, and advancing gender equality.

We are committed to producing high-quality products, promoting sustainable agriculture, and contributing to the economic growth and development of Uganda.

### Vision Statement

To become a leading coffee company in Uganda and Africa, renowned for its exceptional quality, sustainability, and commitment to empowering local coffee growing communities.

### Mission Statement

To produce and deliver high-quality coffee products while promoting sustainable agriculture, fair trade practices, and community development among our partner coffee growers.

## Objectives

Our primary objectives are:

1. **Quality and Sustainability** — Produce high-quality coffee products while adopting environmentally friendly and sustainable agricultural practices, and ensuring high-quality coffee beans and other agricultural products for local and international markets.

2. **Community Development** — Improve the wellbeing of our partner coffee growers and their communities through fair trade practices, training on sustainable agricultural practices, provision of inputs and equipment, and support for community development projects and capacity building.

3. **Market Expansion** — Establish strong market presence in Uganda, Africa, and globally, promoting Ugandan coffee and creating a loyal customer base.

4. **Coffee Buying and Selling** — Source high-quality coffee beans from local farmers and suppliers, providing fair prices and promoting fair trade practices. Hence, promoting sustainable agricultural practices and improving the livelihoods of smallholder farmers, with a focus on women farmers.

5. **Coffee Processing** — Invest in modern coffee processing facilities, ensuring efficient and high-quality processing of coffee beans. Thus, contribute to the economic growth and development of Uganda through job creation, tax revenue, and foreign exchange earnings.

6. **Innovation and Value Addition** — Develop new coffee products and value-added services, such as roasted coffee, ground coffee, and coffee-based beverages, enhancing the customer experience and increasing revenue streams.

7. **Partnerships and Collaborations** — Foster strategic partnerships with our partner coffee growers, suppliers, buyers, and development organisations, both local and international stakeholders, to promote the growth and development of the Ugandan agricultural sector.

## Out-Growers Scheme

Our out-growers scheme is designed to support smallholder farmers in Uganda, with a focus on women farmers. We partner with small-scale coffee growers in Uganda to source high-quality coffee beans.

### The Scheme Provides

1. Training and capacity-building programs on sustainable agricultural practices.
2. Access to high-quality inputs, such as seeds, fertilizers, and agricultural tools and equipment.
3. Technical support and extension services.
4. Market access and fair prices for their produce.

At Africoff Industries (U) Limited, we believe in fair trade practices and ensure that our partner growers receive fair prices for their coffee.

### Benefits of the Out-Growers Scheme

The out-growers' scheme provides numerous benefits to smallholder farmers, including:

1. Improved livelihoods through increased income.
2. Enhanced food security through improved agricultural productivity.
3. Empowerment of women farmers through training and capacity-building programmes.
4. Improved environmental sustainability through the promotion of sustainable agriculture.

### Gender Balance and Women's Empowerment

**Our goals include:**

2. Providing economic opportunities for women farmers and entrepreneurs.
